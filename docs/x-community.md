## How to use these briefs

1. Open a recipe and compare its source preview with the explanation of what changed. The preview illustrates the original post, not the new English brief.
2. Copy the complete English block. These six defaults generate from text and do not require uploading the source image.
3. Replace the named material, product or setting inside the prompt instead of appending contradictory directions. Keep the layout constraints on the first attempt.
4. Check exact text, object counts, perspective and physical connections before using the targeted follow-up.
5. For localized artwork, replace the explicitly quoted text and specify the desired writing system; have a fluent reader check the output. These entries are English prompts, not six newly translated language versions.

## What the references do and do not establish

The X authors identify their work as GPT Image 2.5. We have not independently confirmed their generation settings or reproduced their outputs. P087 uses a post that also describes a video workflow; the adapted deliverable is a static storyboard. P090 describes a transition in a single still image. Neither prompt asserts that the image model directly produces video, audio or a GIF.

The previews are remote links to the authors’ images. They may stop loading, and X may require sign-in; use the original-post and photo links as the source of record. No third-party images have been copied into the original-image asset manifest. All six adapted prompts now have their own original generated examples alongside the separately labeled source previews. See the [gallery](gallery.md) and [generation log](generation-log.md).
