```text
Asset: Portable lamp campaign. Target aspect ratio: 3:2. Mode: generate.

Photograph plausible anodized metal texture and soft shadows.
Constraints: One lamp; no invented battery claims or extra text.
Render only explicitly requested image text. Do not add unrelated logos, signatures or captions.
```

```text

约束：仅一盏灯，不编造续航参数，不添加文字。
```

**Review / 验收：** Check text character by character and compare shade outline and tab placement. 逐字检查文案，比较灯罩轮廓及拉环位置。
